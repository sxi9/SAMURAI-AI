# Avatars

VRM models the HUD can wear when the avatar is set to VRM AVATAR ("avatar mode" by voice). Drop your own `.vrm`
files (VRM 0.x or 1.0) into this folder; they appear in the settings drawer under VRM FILE and can be picked by
voice with "use the avatar <name>".
